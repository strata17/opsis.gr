# Surface: Homepage (src/pages/index.astro) and article template

Scope: the site's front page plus the single-article template. Visitor mode: Read.
Audience: general Greek public, mostly mobile, often arriving from @opsis.gr videos.
Job: see this week's pieces, open one, check its sources. Constraint: every sample piece is labelled sample; numbers only from 07_credits sources.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A front page that behaves like a weekly issue on top of a public register. It refuses the category default of a hero image plus a grid of same-size story cards.
OWN-WORLD: Pentelic marble ground, ink text and hairlines, cinnabar only for the one number that matters, active nav and index numerals. Noto Serif Display headlines, IBM Plex Sans reading text, JetBrains Mono only for source lines, dates and counts. No photos, no shadows, no rounded corners, no cards; structure is ruled lines.
STORY: The reader sees this week's lead and what else is in the issue, understands the claim in one line, sees the source under it, scrolls into the dated register, and trusts that every item can be checked.
FIRST VIEWPORT: ΟΨΙΣ masthead with nav, a mono issue bar (week, dates, number of pieces). Left two-thirds: the lead piece opened, headline at about 64px, dek, source line, then a short ruled table of the facts it rests on. Right third: the contents list of the issue with cinnabar numerals. Primary action: the lead headline link.
FORM: Combination of grounded candidates 3 (register) and 7 (issue), chosen by the user from the dealt hand 3, 2, 7. Seed key 6556d195.
SIGNATURE: The source line; every headline carries ΠΗΓΗ in mono directly under it, and the register sorts by date with sources aligned right. Motion: on load, the hairline rules draw left to right once.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
