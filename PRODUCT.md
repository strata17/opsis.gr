# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
delegated: Astro (static output, Markdown content collections for articles, easy Vercel deploy). Chosen because the site is content-first and needs a blog/news structure that can grow.

## Users
The general Greek public: politically curious readers, mostly arriving from Opsis's TikTok/Instagram videos (@opsis.gr), who want the sourced version of a story. Mostly mobile, reading in Greek.

## Product Purpose
Opsis (ΨΙΣ, tagline «ΚΑΘΑΡΗ ΜΑΤΙΑ», "clean eyes") is a Greek, non-partisan political explainer. It publishes short, data-led takes that separate what the numbers show from the narrative, with sources on every claim. The website is the long-form home for what the short videos can only hint at: full analyses, data, and the complete source list. Success is a reader who trusts the page because every claim can be checked.

## Positioning
Analysis that states plainly what was measured, what it shows and what it does not show, and cites its source on every piece. Opinion is labelled as opinion («ΣΧΟΛΙΟ»), never mixed into data.

## Operating Context
Opsis currently publishes vertical videos (1080x1920) built from one HTML page per video, with a credits/sources comment per video (see 06_video_toolkit and 07_credits). Photos come only from Wikimedia Commons with author and licence noted. Existing content types in the kit: Analysis (ΑΝΑΛΥΣΗ), Data (ΔΕΔΟΜΕΝΑ), Quote, Claim vs Facts («ΛΕΓΕΤΑΙ» / «ΤΑ ΣΤΟΙΧΕΙΑ»), Commentary (ΣΧΟΛΙΟ), Closing with sources.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Language: Greek first. English appears in the kit only as secondary subtitle; bilingual support is undecided.
- Fonts available locally in 04_fonts: Noto Serif Display, IBM Plex Sans, JetBrains Mono (all with Greek subsets).
- Real articles, authors, domain hosting and analytics are undecided.

## Brand Commitments
Binding, from 03_brand_kit/opsis-brand-kit.html ("Marble Edition", marked recommended):
- Wordmark ΨΙΣ with «ΚΑΘΑΡΗ ΜΑΤΙΑ»; O mark with cinnabar pupil (01_logo).
- Palette: Pentelic Marble #E8E6DF, Ink #0F1419, Cinnabar #B5301B (lifted #E0553A on ink), Slate #5A6068, Ash #A5AAB1.
- Type: Noto Serif Display 600 headlines, IBM Plex Sans 400 body, JetBrains Mono for kickers and source lines.
- Source line pattern: ΠΗΓΗ: [ΦΟΡΕΑΣ, ΕΤΟΣ] · ΕΠΕΞΕΡΓΑΣΙΑ: OPSIS.
- Voice: short declarative sentences, conclusion-first headlines.

## Evidence on Hand
Brand kit, logos, fonts, video templates and per-video credits in the repo. No published articles, datasets or testimonials exist yet: sample posts on the site must be clearly marked placeholders and must not present invented statistics as real. Real topics from credits (e.g. ΠΑΣΟΚ/ΕΛΑΣ/ΣΥΡΙΖΑ transfers, 10/10/2026) are factual and may be referenced only as titles with their real sources.

## Product Principles
1. Every claim travels with its source.
2. Data and opinion are visibly separate.
3. Plain, calm, non-partisan tone; no outrage styling.
4. Mobile-first Greek reading experience.
5. The website extends the video brand; it does not reinvent it.
